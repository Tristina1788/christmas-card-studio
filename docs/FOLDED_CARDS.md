# Folded 5 x 7-inch greeting cards

Choose **Download folded PDF** in the editor. This creates a two-page landscape PDF:

| PDF page | Left panel | Right panel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 - outside | Your existing back design (page 4 of the folded card) | Your front design (page 1 of the folded card) |
| 2 - inside | Blank (page 2 of the folded card) | Blank (page 3 of the folded card) |

The inside is blank for a handwritten message. Back text and collage photos remain on the outside back cover. There are no inside editing controls in this version.

Each panel measures exactly 5 x 7 inches. The unfolded card measures 10 x 7 inches; folding it in half produces a portrait 5 x 7-inch card. Existing front/back PNGs and the flat two-page 5 x 7 PDF remain available.

## Paper choices

- **A4 landscape** (297 x 210 mm): the 10 x 7-inch spread is centered on the sheet with crop marks and center fold ticks outside the artwork.
- **US Letter landscape** (11 x 8.5 inches): the same spread is centered with crop marks and fold ticks.
- **10 x 7 inches**: the sheet is exactly the unfolded card size, without guides or margins. Edge-to-edge art requires a printer that supports borderless printing on this stock, or a print shop.

## Print, trim, and fold

1. Open the PDF in a PDF viewer. Select the matching paper size and landscape orientation.
2. Choose **Actual size / 100%**, one PDF page per sheet side. Turn off Fit, Shrink, and booklet/multiple-pages modes. Scaling changes the finished card size.
3. For a blank inside, print **page 1 only**, single-sided. Page 2 is intentionally empty; it has no printed guides or instructions.
4. If printing both PDF pages, enable duplex and **flip on the short edge** for the landscape sheet. Test one sheet to confirm your printer's feed and orientation.
5. On A4 or Letter, note the center fold position using the two ticks above and below the artwork. Trim the outside spread to the four corner crop marks: it should measure 10 x 7 inches (254 x 177.8 mm). Fold halfway across the 10-inch width, at 5 inches (127 mm), with the artwork facing out. The front cover is the right panel when the outside is viewed upright.
6. On exact 10 x 7-inch stock, simply fold at the 5-inch center. The finished card should measure 5 x 7 inches (127 x 177.8 mm).

The center ticks stay outside the final trimmed card, so no dashed line appears on the artwork. Lightly mark or score the center before trimming if helpful. Use cardstock compatible with your printer. Measure and fold a plain-paper test before printing a batch.

This export adds no bleed. It uses the same RGB canvas artwork as the preview and PNG downloads; it is not a CMYK or commercial bleed-ready file. Ask your print shop for its requirements if needed.
